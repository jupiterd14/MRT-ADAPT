Travel Plan Guide

1

Select Your Stations

Choose your departure station (FROM) and destination station (TO). The congestion level will be displayed below each dropdown.

2

Set Your Travel Time

Pick your travel date and departure time. The system will show congestion forecasts for your selected time.

3

Plan Your Route

Click the PLAN button to get travel advisories, estimated travel time, and congestion levels for your chosen route.

4

Read the Advisories

Three advisory cards will show: boarding station status, travel recommendations, and ETA with congestion alerts.

Tip: Use the swap button to quickly exchange FROM and TO stations. Click "X" to reset all selections.
